01 • Project

From observation to action

The internship journey started with campus sustainability observation, moved through data cleaning and visualization, and evolved into a smart waste-management workflow.

Observe

Document campus waste and sustainability issues directly from the campus environment.

Analyze

Clean records, compare waste types and locations, and identify high-generation patterns.

Act

Use priority levels to support better collection, segregation and future prediction.

03 • AI Workflow

Prediction-ready workflow

Inputs

Date/time, campus location, waste type, quantity, previous collection status and recent trends.

Prediction

The concept can estimate near-term waste quantity or classify a location as High / Medium / Low priority.

Action

Use the priority to decide collection order, then record the actual result for feedback.
